Excellon DMS and Web DMS

A dealer management system that runs a vehicle dealership: leads, quotes, service, parts, finance and e-invoicing in one place. I redesigned the experience from outdated screens to a modern, consistent product.

Role
UI/UX designer, end to end
Scope
12 modules and sub-modules
Platforms
DMS and Web DMS
Tools
Figma, Figma AI, Claude

The problem

Dealership staff moved across many modules to qualify a lead, build a quote and follow up. Screens were dated and looked different in every module, so each one had to be relearned.

My process

  1. Ran discovery with stakeholders and mapped the lead-to-quote flow.
  2. Defined page patterns for lists, forms and detail views.
  3. Built a library of 500+ icons and components.
  4. Designed the 12 modules on those patterns.
  5. Reviewed with developers and refined for hand-off.

Key decisions

  • Status chips pair a dot with a label, so meaning is not colour alone.
  • Leads switch between table and card views.
  • A right rail keeps progress, pending tasks and quick actions in view.
  • The amount summary collapses to give quote tables room.

Outcome

One visual language across 12 modules, implementation-ready files for developers, and a usability gain that contributed to 40% business growth.

Beyond Physique Fit: a 12-week coaching program site

A conversion-focused landing page and application flow for a fitness coaching program, from first promise to secure payment.

Role
Design and front-end build
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Vercel
Payments
Razorpay
Status
Live, 62 commits
bpf-beyond-physique-fit.vercel.app

The problem

A program with a Rs 99 entry offer and two premium plans needed a page that explains the offer, builds trust and gets people through application and payment on a phone.

My approach

  1. Lead with one promise and one action: 12 weeks, real change, Rs 99.
  2. Build trust with a before-and-after results gallery.
  3. Show the three plans side by side so the price ladder is clear.
  4. Split the application into 3 short steps with a progress bar.
  5. Hand off to Razorpay; program materials arrive by email.

Key decisions

  • Short steps keep each screen to a few fields.
  • A soft exit prompt lets people continue later instead of leaving.
  • Every call to action points to the same application.

Outcome

A working end-to-end funnel in production: landing page, 3-step application, secure payment and automated delivery of materials.
